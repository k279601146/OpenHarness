# QA and Optional Extensions

## Pre-presentation QA

Fail and revise if any answer is “no”:

- Was the approved Blueprint image used, and is the current artifact inside its system?
- Was the exact approved Master/Variant Slot reused rather than redrawn?
- Are canvas, posture, frame/window/UI treatment, object scale/position, margins, type, color roles, and orientation consistent?
- Does every slot provide unique proof without repeated feature rows, icon strips, badges, or microcopy?
- Is all marketing text readable at thumbnail size with a clear first focal point?
- Is supplied UI/copy/brand truth preserved and conceptual content disclosed?
- Are claims, social proof, prices, awards, ranks, and reviews verified rather than invented?
- Is the correct platform convention used instead of an Apple-phone template everywhere?
- Are Android captions minor, UI authentic, restricted CTA/promotion language absent, and required Feature Graphic/TV banner present?
- Is the icon recognizable small, free of baked text/fine detail, and coherent with the listing system?
- Are edge crops intentional and overlays/safe areas anticipated?
- Has the user approved each required gate through `ask_human`?
- Are current console specs flagged for live verification rather than claimed as guaranteed compliance?

## ASO copy — only when requested

Produce per platform and locale:

- app name/title and subtitle/short description within current console limits;
- concise positioning statement and long description structured by benefit → proof → feature → trust;
- keyword/theme recommendations based on truthful product capabilities;
- localization notes for intent, expansion, cultural fit, and protected names.

Avoid keyword stuffing, unsupported superlatives, duplicated screenshot messages, decorative punctuation, and promises the product cannot prove. For Google Play short description, follow the 80-character and policy constraints in `platform-routing.md`.

## Preview-video script — only when requested

Provide a scene table with timecode, authentic UI/action, on-screen copy, motion/transition, audio/VO, and platform crop/localization notes. Open on product value, show real use early, and preserve the locked listing visual DNA without turning still screenshots into a slideshow.

For Google Play, plan delivery as an eligible YouTube link and anticipate a play overlay on the Feature Graphic. For Apple, verify current App Preview duration, codec, device/posture, and upload rules before final export. Never claim the script itself is a submission-ready encoded video.
