# Campaigns, Posters and Social Images

Use for promotion, launch, banners and product-based social carousels. Reuse the requested channel, audience, message, count and brand direction. Do not add a strategy workshop, compulsory style options or a fixed card sequence. Existing localization uses [image-translation.md](image-translation.md).

## Choose message hierarchy and composition

| Task | Design mechanism | Write into the prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Promotion | Offer leads; the product explains what it applies to; conditions remain near the offer and readable | "Make '[exact offer]' the primary text. Show [eligible product] beside it. Keep '[dates/scope/threshold]' together as supporting copy at [location]." |
| Product launch | Product plus one supported distinction; a detail or use scene supplies evidence | "Show [actual product] in [evidenced state/context]. Emphasize [feature] through [visible detail/action]. Place '[exact launch copy]' in [quiet region]." |
| Product-led social cover | A recognizable use moment or feature answers the opening question | "Show [user/action/context] with [product interaction] visible. Place '[short exact headline]' beside the visual evidence without covering [key feature]." |
| Buying guide or explanation | Aligned rows for options/facts; photo-led evidence when inspection matters | "Group [verified options] into [rows/columns], each with its corresponding [photo/value/copy]. Keep [comparison basis] consistent." |
| Brand/lifestyle story | One clear scene plus concise message; let the asset carry mood | "Use [requested scene and treatment] to support '[exact message]'. Keep [product/brand asset] recognizable and make [interaction] the focal event." |

Use image/text split when copy is too long or the photo lacks a quiet region. Overlay suits short copy with enough contrast and an unobscured subject. Editorial photo-and-caption, aligned information rows, or a number-led offer are options chosen by content, not by nationality or category stereotype.

Use [visual-recipes.md](visual-recipes.md) for a concrete type/effect direction. Optional decoration should reinforce the message or group information. Apply conceptual material effects to background or display lettering when appropriate; changing the merchandise itself into glass, candy or fabric requires an explicit concept request.

Name a concrete font class in the prompt rather than "modern clean font" or a default heavy sans. For CJK copy, pick the class from the campaign's register instead of a flat 黑体: 宋体/Ming serif for editorial or premium-culture tone; 仿宋 for specification captions; 楷体 for handmade or gifting warmth; 魏碑 for bold heritage statements; brush calligraphy for festival creative — at most one line per image, paired with a 黑体/宋体 supporting line; 隶书 for traditional food or herbal tone; 黑体 suits tech, appliance or parameter-led layouts.

Hold one simplified/traditional variant across the campaign and require intact, undistorted glyphs: no fake bold or italic, no mechanically stretched characters, no letter-spaced Chinese headlines. For a multi-image campaign, reuse the set's type clause verbatim in every text-bearing page and vary placement, size and contrast instead; see [design-patterns.md](design-patterns.md).

## Social carousel: build the requested story

Assign each requested page a distinct question or job, using available evidence: recognition/hook, use context, feature explanation, fit/selection, or action. Keep the user's order if given. Do not require nine cards, a before/after comparison, an emotional climax, a divider, or a CTA. A two-page task remains two pages.

For each page resolve: **message -> supporting scene/detail -> product interaction -> exact copy -> layout**. A usage benefit becomes an observable event, not a generic happy person holding an unrelated item. Keep the product visible where its benefit is explained; do not substitute mood for evidence.

Share palette, brand assets, type hierarchy and image treatment; vary page layout with purpose. A hero scene and a detail page can share identity without sharing a camera shot. Existing assets/textual style allow independent generation through [parallel-generation.md](parallel-generation.md); do not wait for card 1 merely because it is first. A caller-required accepted style image remains a real dependency.

## Prompt pattern

"Asset type: [campaign role]. Input images: [product identity; brand artwork; optional style reference]. Primary request: Communicate [supported message] through [specific scene/detail/action]. Canvas and composition: [chosen structure and product/text relationship]. Materials, color and style: [shared visual treatment]. Typography: Render '[headline]' as the primary message and '[qualifying copy]' at [readable location]. Product invariants: [actual identity and offer facts]."

Omit absent copy rather than inventing it. Examples in designer libraries are not prices, campaign dates, testimonials or performance evidence for this merchant. Keep a qualifier such as "up to", eligibility or minimum spend bound to the advertised offer.

## Produce and inspect

Generate a full composition when its text precision and volume suit the available tool. For dense terms, exact prices, actual logos or QR codes, use available native composition when useful while delivering the expected image. A supplied QR code must stay intact; a new functional code requires a provided destination and a suitable encoder, not drawn squares from an image model.

Inspect reading order at intended viewing size, complete offer conditions, product fidelity, believable contact and per-page message coverage. Preserve conversation language separately from artwork language. Captions, ad publishing, invented urgency and extra campaign variants are outside the requested image deliverables unless authorized.
