# Career, Company, and Finance Public Research Route

Use this route for public hiring research, company research, competitor research, market research, and finance/industry public information.

## Scope

- Public company pages
- Public job postings
- Public career pages
- Public pricing and product pages
- Public annual reports, investor relations pages, exchange disclosures, and news
- Public finance/community pages when reachable without login

## Process

1. Define the target: role, company, competitor, stock/fund/industry, geography, and time range.
2. Collect public source links.
3. Extract facts, requirements, signals, and dates.
4. Compare multiple sources when possible.
5. Mark uncertain or stale information.

## Output

- Public source table
- Role/company/product/market summary
- Trends and repeated signals
- Risks and caveats
- Suggested next checks

## Boundary

Do not access private profiles, private messages, private job dashboards, paid databases, login-only finance feeds, or account-specific data. Do not ask for credentials, cookies, or proxy settings.
